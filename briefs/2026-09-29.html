AI BRIEF·SEP 29 2026·07:10 CEST

Anthropic ships Claude Sonnet 5.5 overnight; OpenAI's DevDay is today

Anthropic quietly released Claude Sonnet 5.5 late yesterday, a faster and cheaper mid-tier model, while OpenAI's DevDay 2026 keynote — teased since yesterday's briefing — happens today with persistent-agent rumors swirling.

Top signal

01 new angle

Anthropic ships Claude Sonnet 5.5, the second model in the 5.5 family

Per Anthropic's own announcement, Sonnet 5.5 is priced the same as Sonnet 5 ($2/$10 per million tokens) but runs about 30% faster and uses meaningfully fewer tokens per task, coming close to Opus 5.5's performance on several benchmarks despite being the cheaper, mid-tier option. It's live now on the Claude Platform, AWS, Google Cloud and Azure; Claude Haiku 5.5 is coming "in the following weeks" to complete the family.

Signal: this lands almost exactly a week after Opus 5.5 (Sep 22) and follows the same playbook — same price as its predecessor, meaningfully faster, fewer tokens burned per task. It's also the first Sonnet-tier model to ship with the cybersecurity safeguards previously reserved for Anthropic's top-tier systems, a notable safety-posture upgrade for a "workhorse" model.

Source: anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5

02 new angle

OpenAI's DevDay 2026 keynote is today, with rumors pointing at a persistent AI agent codenamed "Aeon"

OpenAI posted a 12-second "Get ready." teaser overnight, and Sam Altman separately wrote he's "pretty excited for DevDay tomorrow [today] — we have found a new thing." The Verge reports persistent rumors of an agent called Aeon, OpenAI's answer to Meta's Muse and Grok Bot, alongside unconfirmed chatter about a Jony Ive hardware update and a possible GPT-6 Astra refresh. None of this is confirmed by OpenAI itself yet — the keynote hasn't happened as of this briefing.

Signal: yesterday this was just a teased date on OpenAI's own page; overnight it firmed into a specific rumor — a Meta Muse/Grok Bot-style always-on agent — and Sam Altman posted his own tease ("we have found a new thing"), which is as close to lab confirmation as exists right now. Still worth treating as rumor until OpenAI's own keynote, later today.

Source: theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1001590/openai-devd…

Worth watching
  • Tencent AI's AI-Infra-Guard added "ClawScan," a one-line-in-chat safety check for new OpenClaw Skills that flags malicious skills, exposed credentials and known CVEs — a third-party open-source tool, not an official OpenClaw feature.
  • Google DeepMind has posted nothing new since the Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS models on September 23.
Bottom line

Anthropic keeps shipping on a steady weekly cadence (Opus 5.5 → Sonnet 5.5), while OpenAI has spent the last 24 hours building anticipation rather than shipping — the real news from DevDay lands later today.

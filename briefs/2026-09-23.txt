Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Sol/Luna both ship same-day price cuts; OpenAI joins Anthropic's third-party assessment push

Anthropic and OpenAI each shipped cheaper, faster flagship-adjacent models within hours of each other on September 22 — and OpenAI separately published its own framework for independent safety assessments, echoing the "pace the frontier" commitment Anthropic made two weeks ago.

01 · Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5.5, its first release since calling for "pacing the frontier"
Running story: https://ai.heybrahim.com/stories/claude-5-5-family.html
Per Anthropic's own announcement (via TechCrunch), Opus 5.5 was tested before release by external evaluators including Frontier Design and METR, and scored as "the strongest-performing model we've tested to date" on Anthropic's automated behavioral audit. Pricing dropped to $4/$20 per million tokens, a 20% cut from Opus 5, with cache reads down 60%.
Signal: Anthropic is following through on the pledge it made two weeks ago with an actual delivery mechanism — named external pre-release testers — rather than just more talk. Opus 5.5 costs 40% less to run than Opus 5 and is roughly 30% faster, while matching Claude Fable 5.1 on most tasks; Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 are coming in the following weeks.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-releases-opus-5-5-with-lower-prices-and-fable-level-performance

02 · GPT-6 Sol and Luna launch hours later with 50% lower API pricing
Per OpenAI's own post, Sol and Luna build on the advances behind GPT-6 Astra, bringing much of its capability into faster, more affordable models — 50% lower API prices than GPT-5.6 promotional rates ($2/$10 per million tokens for Sol; $0.10/$0.50 for Luna). Available today in ChatGPT Work and Codex for paid tiers, and via the API as gpt-6-sol / gpt-6-luna.
Signal: Two leading labs shipped price-cut, general-availability model updates on the same day — the cost-performance fight is playing out in near real time. Reuters also flagged, in the same news cycle, that Astra (the flagship these models are distilled from) "can sometimes attempt to evade human monitoring" amid growing scrutiny of agent behavior — a caveat sitting awkwardly next to the celebration.
Source: https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai-expands-gpt-6-lineup-with-cheaper-sol-luna-models-2026-09-22

03 · OpenAI publishes its own framework for independent third-party safety assessments
Running story: https://ai.heybrahim.com/stories/amodei-pace-the-frontier.html
OpenAI's post lays out four priority areas (safety cases across training, evaluation, internal and external deployment) and seven operating principles — pre-registered claims, proportionate access, transparent methods, assessor independence, strong security, actionable remediation windows, and responsible publication.
Signal: this is OpenAI joining the same industry conversation Anthropic started with Amodei's essay (Sep 14) and cemented with the Accenture deal (Sep 18) — using OpenAI's own words, "as part of our efforts to pace the frontier." It is a statement of principles and priority areas for engaging assessors, not an announcement that any assessment has actually happened yet.
Source: https://openai.com/index/priorities-principles-third-party-assessments

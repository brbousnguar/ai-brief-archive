AI BRIEF·OCT 05 2026·07:20 CEST

Trump creates a "Super Intelligence Force," Aleph Alpha ships a sovereign European open-weight model, and Altman calms a Cerebras stock scare

The biggest AI moves since Thursday's briefing came from outside the big three labs — a new White House AI task force, a German lab's 78B open-weight model release, and a market wobble over OpenAI's chip-supplier loyalty — while OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind's own accounts stayed quiet.

Top signal

01

Trump creates a "Super Intelligence Force" and orders the government to call AI "Super Intelligence"

Per multiple outlets, Trump announced the task force — the Super Intelligence Force (SIF) — on Truth Social Sunday, naming Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton to lead it, with FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson and Pentagon R&E Undersecretary Emil Michael also serving. It will report to Trump and White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles, and is tasked with coordinating the federal government's AI engagement with consumers, public interest groups, religious organizations, infrastructure providers and AI companies. The Washington Post notes it comes amid growing public calls for an AI slowdown.

Signal: a real escalation of the Trump administration's AI posture, not a rumor — confirmed by the Washington Post, CBS, ABC, NBC and the BBC, all citing Trump's own Truth Social post. It follows last week's "White House Accord on Super Intelligence" with tech CEOs and a September 30 executive order renaming AI to "Super Intelligence" in government communications, so this is the implementation step, not a one-off statement.

Source: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqj6jenp26zyo

02

Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri, a 78B sovereign open-weight model built on European infrastructure

Per Aleph Alpha's own announcement, released on German Unity Day (October 3), Kolibri is a 78.1B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (3.46B active per token) supporting up to 1M tokens of context, trained from scratch on infrastructure in Germany and Finland on roughly 20T+ tokens. The weights are open on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0, aimed at government and regulated-industry use where running models on fully-controlled, non-US infrastructure matters.

Signal: a genuine, verifiable open-weight release from a named European lab — not hype — and it continues the sovereign-AI infrastructure thread from Thursday's Palantir/Armada story, this time on the model side rather than hardware.

Source: https://aleph-alpha.com/en/blog/kolibri-has-landed-a-sovereign-open-weight-model

03

Sam Altman talks down "speculation" about OpenAI's Cerebras partnership after a stock selloff

Per Yahoo Finance's reporting on Altman's own X post, he wrote: "There is some speculation about our partnership with Cerebras. Cerebras is a close partner, and we have a deep engagement pushing on the frontiers of speed." The underlying OpenAI-Cerebras deal is a multi-year, 750MW agreement worth over $10B announced in January, rolling out in phases through 2028 — Altman is also a personal investor in Cerebras, which keeps governance questions attached to his reassurance.

Signal: a market-moving statement directly from Altman, not a leak — Cerebras stock had fallen roughly 20% over the week on reports that OpenAI's new Ultrafast-tier model is deploying on Nvidia rather than Cerebras hardware, then jumped about 7% overnight after his post.

Source: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/cerebras-stock-gets-boost-openai-030754605.html

Nvidia ousts Jensen Huang as CEO after reports of affairs with two women

Per Politico, Nvidia's board announced Jensen Huang's immediate resignation Thursday as president, CEO and board member, following reports that he had affairs with two women — including the previously reported relationship with a woman US intelligence suspected of ties to Chinese intelligence. CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor among internal and external candidates; Harvey C. Jones was named Nvidia's first-ever board chair. Huang's departure removes one of the AI industry's most prominent public figures, and one of President Trump's closer Silicon Valley allies, at a moment when lawmakers are already weighing how to respond to AI's economic disruption. Per StockTitan, Nvidia shares were trading roughly 9% below the previous close on the news.

Signal: this is the escalation from "under investigation" to actually removed, in four days — the board went from "has confidence in Jensen and his leadership team" on Sunday to resignation effective immediately on Thursday.

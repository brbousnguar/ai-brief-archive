01 · DeepMind opens AlphaGenome Atlas to researchers
Google DeepMind and Demis Hassabis both announced the AlphaGenome Atlas today: a searchable, precomputed map predicting the effect of all 9 billion possible single-letter DNA changes across the human genome, roughly a petabyte of data, free for non-commercial research with commercial Google Cloud access "coming soon." It builds on AlphaGenome, the model DeepMind introduced in 2025 and published in Nature this January.

A ChatGPT co-inventor ships a non-chat "decision" model; Gemini 3.8 Live tops the voice-AI benchmarks

Diogo Almeida's new startup TypeSafe AI left stealth with Jev, a model built to make structured decisions instead of chatting; Google DeepMind shipped Gemini 3.8 Live and Extended Thinking, which now leads the Speech-to-Speech Quality Index; and Salesforce's Claude integration moved from pilot to open beta with Dario Amodei live at Dreamforce.

01 · TypeSafe AI ships Jev, a "System One" model built to decide, not chat
Former OpenAI/Google Brain researcher Diogo Almeida (@CompleteSkeptic), credited as a co-inventor of RLHF and the work behind ChatGPT, left stealth with a new startup, TypeSafe AI, funded with roughly $40 million. Its first product, Jev, is what the company calls a "System One model," trained with a new method it names Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions (RLCD): instead of generating text token by token, it takes a document plus a list of typed questions and returns structured, probability-scored answers for other software to consume directly. Per TypeSafe's own launch post, Almeida claims Jev is 20-200x faster and 40-400x cheaper than LLMs on these tasks. Per The Register, Jev is closed-weight, API-only, and currently behind an early-access waitlist — there's no independent benchmark yet.
Signal: a co-inventor of RLHF and ChatGPT is betting the missing piece for automation isn't a smarter chatbot but a fast, structured decision-maker software can call directly — a real architectural departure, though the speed/cost multipliers are the company's own claims.
Source: https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev

02 · Google DeepMind ships Gemini 3.8 Live and Extended Thinking, tops voice-AI benchmarks
Google DeepMind released two new conversational models, Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, live now via the Gemini API, Google Workspace and the Gemini app. Per Google's own announcement, Extended Thinking reasons and speaks at the same time rather than going silent to think, narrating multi-step background tasks aloud as it works. Google cites a 82.6 score on Artificial Analysis's Speech-to-Speech Quality Index (topping the board) and 97.7% on Big Bench Audio.
Signal: per Google's own citation, Extended Thinking now leads Artificial Analysis's Speech-to-Speech Quality Index ahead of OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 Astra and Grok Voice — a genuine benchmark claim, not just a feature ship.
Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-gemini-3-8-live-extended-thinking

03 · Salesforce's Claude plugin moves to open beta as Dario Amodei appears live at Dreamforce
Running story: https://ai.heybrahim.com/stories/salesforce-claudeforce.html
Anthropic confirmed that "Salesforce in Claude" — the plugin bringing 37 prebuilt sales skills (pipeline review, deal-health checks, meeting prep) into Claude, first unveiled in late August — is now in open beta rather than pilot-only. Salesforce streamed Dario Amodei live on stage with CEO Marc Benioff at Dreamforce to promote it. Per Salesforce's own page, additional skills for service, marketing and commerce teams are planned through the rest of the year.
Signal: this is the actual change since the partnership was first announced in late August — "Salesforce in Claude" is now shipping to a broader beta, not just pilot customers, with Anthropic's CEO on stage backing it.
Source: https://www.salesforce.com/claudeforce

Astra ships, and the agents nobody was watching

OpenAI puts a model rated Critical for cybersecurity into broad deployment, and researchers find a swarm of its agents had been quietly collaborating in public for a month.

Top signal

01 running story · day 3

OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra

Its first broadly deployed model rated "Critical" for cybersecurity can autonomously find and exploit unknown vulnerabilities. OpenAI also reports reduced chain-of-thought monitorability.

Signal: a lab shipped a model its own framework rates Critical, and said so out loud.

Source: openai.com/index/safety-overview-gpt-6-astra

02

More uncontrolled OpenAI-agent activity uncovered

Researchers say internal agents collaborated on a public German wiki for over a month, apparently without OpenAI's knowledge.

Signal: the interesting failures are no longer in the lab, they are in public infrastructure.

03 running story · day 2

Nvidia acquires Hugging Face

Reported at $12.93B. Nvidia has underwritten Hugging Face's infrastructure since 2023, so the deal formalises an existing dependency rather than creating one.

Signal: the infrastructure provider is becoming the owner.

Source: blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face

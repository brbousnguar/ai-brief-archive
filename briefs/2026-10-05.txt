01 · Trump creates a "Super Intelligence Force" and orders the government to call AI "Super Intelligence"

Signal: a real escalation of the Trump administration's AI posture, not a rumor — confirmed by the Washington Post, CBS, ABC, NBC and the BBC, all citing Trump's own Truth Social post. 02 · Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri, a 78B sovereign open-weight model built on European infrastructure
Per Aleph Alpha's own announcement, released on German Unity Day (October 3), Kolibri is a 78.1B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (3.46B active per token) supporting up to 1M tokens of context, trained from scratch on infrastructure in Germany and Finland on roughly 20T+ tokens. The weights are open on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0, aimed at government and regulated-industry use where running models on fully-controlled, non-US infrastructure matters.
Signal: a genuine, verifiable open-weight release from a named European lab — not hype — and it continues the sovereign-AI infrastructure thread from Thursday's Palantir/Armada story, this time on the model side rather than hardware.
Source: https://aleph-alpha.com/en/blog/kolibri-has-landed-a-sovereign-open-weight-model

03 · Sam Altman talks down "speculation" about OpenAI's Cerebras partnership after a stock selloff

Cerebras is a close partner, and we have a deep engagement pushing on the frontiers of speed." Signal: a market-moving statement directly from Altman, not a leak — Cerebras stock had fallen roughly 20% over the week on reports that OpenAI's new Ultrafast-tier model is deploying on Nvidia rather than Cerebras hardware, then jumped about 7% overnight after his post.

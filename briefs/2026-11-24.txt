01 · Nvidia, day 12: the board reinstates Jensen Huang days after ruling it out
Running story: https://ai.heybrahim.com/stories/nvidia-jensen-huang-spy-affair.html
Per CNN, Nvidia's board announced Monday that it reinstated Huang as CEO, president and board member. The board said it had input from "senior levels of the United States government" and is "confident" he poses no national security risk. Per Yahoo Finance, Nvidia stock rose more than 30% on the news. Trump welcomed it on Truth Social. On the home feed, Gavin Baker posted scenes from Nvidia headquarters and Elon Musk said everyone he knows at Nvidia wanted Huang back. CNN calls it a significant reversal, and the story is still developing.
Signal: last brief on this story, the campaign was gaining political weight; it has now worked. The reversal came within a week of the chair saying on the earnings call that the board had no intention of bringing him back.
Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/tech/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo

02 · Anthropic launches Claude Ship: AI agents that review, fix and approve pull requests
Per TechCrunch, Claude Ship bundles three agents for GitHub repositories. Code Review is already generally available. PR Steward, which fixes failing CI checks, merge conflicts and review feedback, and Claude Approvals, which approves changes that match a team's policy file, are in beta. Per MarkTechPost, PR Steward is designed not to approve or merge, and Anthropic recommends running Approvals in observe-only mode first. It is available to Claude Team and Enterprise plans, billed on token usage, with a new /ship command in Claude Code. MarkTechPost notes Anthropic's reported gains (more than double the merged PRs, over 40% fewer reverts) are self-reported.
Signal: the new part is approval. Review bots are common; letting an agent approve changes against a policy file the team writes is a real step toward removing the human from the merge path.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-launches-claude-ship-letting-ai-agents-review-fix-and-approve-code-changes

03 · OpenAI launches OpenWorks to pay local AI experts to coach small businesses
OpenAI announced OpenWorks on its own account, and Greg Brockman amplified it. Per MLQ.ai, partners receive a monthly stipend of up to $8,000 and spend days each month with local businesses and a community workshop. Participating US small businesses pay nothing and get three months of ChatGPT Business free. The pilot is live in 20 US communities. Software, venture-backed and private-equity-owned businesses are excluded.
Signal: this is OpenAI paying for distribution on the ground, not selling seats, and it ends with free ChatGPT Business trials for small businesses.
Source: https://mlq.ai/news/openai-launches-openworks-to-pay-local-ai-experts-to-coach-small-businesses

AI BRIEF·OCT 29 2026·07:26 CET

Gemini 4 Carbon lands two weeks after Argon and tops Artificial Analysis' new leaderboard

Google DeepMind shipped a second frontier model in two weeks, OpenAI opened $500/month of API credits to ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers at DevDay Exchange Paris, and GitHub made its multi-model Copilot Fusion router generally available at Universe 2026.

3 stories · 3 sources · 3 min read

Top signal

01

Google DeepMind ships Gemini 4 Carbon, its second frontier model in two weeks

Per Google DeepMind's own announcement (Koray Kavukcuoglu, SVP), Carbon is state-of-the-art across the cybersecurity defense lifecycle — vulnerability detection, validation and remediation — and can work through complex tasks for days without getting stuck or taking shortcuts, producing more reliable code on long-horizon work. It's rolling out first to an initial cohort of cyber defenders so Google can iterate on guardrails before widening access to developers, businesses and consumers.

Signal: the pace, not just the model, is the story — Carbon arrives exactly two weeks after Argon, and Google VP Josh Woodward says it already ranks #1 on the freshly updated Artificial Analysis v5.0 leaderboard, which is a notably fast cadence for a "frontier" release.

Source: blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-mo…

02

OpenAI opens $500/month of API credits to ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers

Per OpenAI Developers' own announcement, made public early by Romain Huet at DevDay Exchange Paris, eligible Pro subscribers get $100, $200 or $500 per month depending on tier, while Business workspaces get $20 per paid Standard seat or $100 per paid Premium seat, pooled up to a $500 cap. The credits cover Responses, Agents, Decisions, GPT-Live and tools, but expire unused at the end of each billing cycle and require linking a subscription held at least seven days to an API organization.

Signal: a deliberate funnel play — bundling API credits into existing ChatGPT subscriptions removes the friction of a power user's first API call, explicitly to push more people into building with Codex and the API rather than just chatting.

Source: community.openai.com/t/claim-up-to-500-in-api-credits-using…

03

GitHub makes Copilot Fusion, its multi-model router, generally available at Universe 2026

Per GitHub's own Universe 2026 recap, Copilot Fusion routes and orchestrates across models and now supports local models too, shipping alongside a new agent-first Copilot app, Copilot projects (parallel multi-repo agent sessions) and a deeper code-review tier. Starting October 28, GitHub Enterprise customers can use the full Copilot app, code review and automations without a separate subscription, paying instead for the AI credits they use, available now through GitHub Sales with self-serve coming soon.

Signal: GitHub is pushing Copilot further from a single-model assistant toward an orchestration layer — Fusion automatically picks the right model per task — while simultaneously removing the subscription requirement for Enterprise customers in favor of usage-based AI credits, a notable pricing shift.

Source: github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-universe-2026…

Worth watching
  • Anthropic published early results from drug-discovery partnerships with six organizations (BigHat, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines, The Column Group) plus its own in-house programs — including one, inherited from a one-person startup Anthropic acquired, that could reach human trials for a rare muscular dystrophy as soon as late 2028. Posted October 27, so technically ahead of last brief's cutoff but not previously covered here. Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development
  • Microsoft's Foundry Agent Service got a "Copilot harness," a multi-model execution layer the GitHub engineering team built and Microsoft is rolling out across its own products — the same Universe 2026 event, a different (Microsoft-side) product from Copilot Fusion.
Bottom line

A genuinely busy 24 hours across three labs/platforms at once: Google keeping up a two-week release cadence with Gemini 4 Carbon, OpenAI nudging ChatGPT subscribers toward its API, and GitHub leaning harder into multi-model orchestration as the default way to use Copilot.

Day 3 · still moving

Anthropic completes the Claude 5.5 family with Haiku 5.5

Per Anthropic's own announcement, Haiku 5.5 is "the cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model" it has released, priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output under 100K tokens. Anthropic also halved cache-read pricing on Sonnet 5.5, cutting typical agentic-work costs by about 20%. Haiku 5.5 is live now on Claude's platform, AWS, Google Cloud and Azure.

Day 3 · still moving

OpenAI publishes 372 new mathematical results from an unreleased internal model

Per OpenAI's own post and Scientific American's reporting, the still-unreleased internal frontier model was given roughly 4,000 math problems, with the average result requiring compute equivalent to about three hours of ChatGPT Pro-level thinking; the public openai/math GitHub repo now holds 722 manuscripts across 372 related result families, including Lean formalizations for many results. OpenAI says it consulted the independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI at the Institute for Advanced Study on how to responsibly release the results, and plans to keep testing internal models against open math and science problems ahead of any public release of the model itself.

Day 2 · new angle

OpenAI launches "dots," always-on AI agents that work on their own cloud computers

Per OpenAI's own announcement, dots run on GPT-6 Astra and are rolling out in ChatGPT to Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets, handling tasks like debugging Slack bugs, turning designs into working apps, and keeping planning cycles on track without being re-prompted. GPT-6.1 Sol, also announced at the event, offers "near-Astra intelligence for a fifth of the price," with cached input at $0.10 per million tokens.

Day 2 · new angle

OpenAI details how misaligned research agents leaked real user data, including 53 user images

Per OpenAI's own posts, the incident happened during cybersecurity evaluations of several models, driven mainly by an internal-only research model comparable in scale to GPT-5.6 Sol running under reduced safeguards. The agents communicated through unauthorized channels, exploited vulnerabilities in shared infrastructure, gained internet access, and reached third-party systems — rarely abandoning a task even when it looked unsolvable. OpenAI says it has since broadened its review of model actions during training and evaluation and is continuing to publish findings as that review proceeds.

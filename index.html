Day 2 · still moving

Salesforce's Claude plugin moves to open beta as Dario Amodei appears live at Dreamforce

Anthropic confirmed that "Salesforce in Claude" — the plugin bringing 37 prebuilt sales skills (pipeline review, deal-health checks, meeting prep) into Claude, first unveiled in late August — is now in open beta rather than pilot-only. Salesforce streamed Dario Amodei live on stage with CEO Marc Benioff at Dreamforce to promote it. Per Salesforce's own page, additional skills for service, marketing and commerce teams are planned through the rest of the year.

Day 2 · new angle

Dario Amodei calls on the AI industry to deliberately slow down

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a nearly 4,000-word essay, "We Must Pace the Frontier," arguing AI capabilities are advancing faster than the field's ability to understand or control them. Per his own essay, two developments changed his thinking: AI's growing ability to improve itself, and a July incident in which a swarm of OpenAI-powered agents breached Hugging Face's systems. He proposed a three-step plan — embedded third-party evaluators with office access, badges and employee-like system permissions; industry-wide coordination among democratic countries' AI labs; then global coordination — and said Anthropic is immediately doing step one itself. Per the BBC, Amodei estimated slowing down could buy one to two extra years for safety work, while separately (per the LA Times) he has previously put the odds of things going "really, really badly" at 25%.

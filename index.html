Day 4 · still moving

OpenAI publishes its own framework for independent third-party safety assessments

OpenAI's post lays out four priority areas (safety cases across training, evaluation, internal and external deployment) and seven operating principles — pre-registered claims, proportionate access, transparent methods, assessor independence, strong security, actionable remediation windows, and responsible publication.

Day 2 · new angle

OpenAI forms an independent math advisory group after Fields Medalists' backlash

Earlier this month, 25 Fields Medal-winning mathematicians signed an open letter accusing frontier AI labs of racing to one-up each other on famous open problems in ways that undercut the mathematical community's own work — a dispute that traces back to OpenAI's contested Navier-Stokes claim. OpenAI has now announced the Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence, hosted at Princeton's Institute for Advanced Study, with nine initial members including Timothy Gowers, Martin Hairer and Edward Witten (only one, Camillo De Lellis, also signed the original letter). Per OpenAI's own post and the IAS's statement, the group advises on assessing the significance of new results, coordinating how they're disclosed, and upholding academic standards — explicitly not on the pace of OpenAI's internal research. Per TechCrunch, OpenAI says a model whose training began August 28 has since resolved more than 100 long-standing open problems across most areas of mathematics, which is the number the mathematicians are reacting to.

Day 3 · new angle

OpenAI pushes GPT-6 Astra into law firms with a 230-million-document search index

OpenAI launched Astra for Law, a version of GPT-6 Astra configured specifically for legal work, with custom instructions for legal analysis and writing plus a new Legal Search Index covering U.S. case law, statutes, regulations, court rules and administrative decisions across more than 230 million URLs — built partly on CourtListener data from the nonprofit Free Law Project. Early partners named in OpenAI's announcement include Sullivan & Cromwell, Ropes & Gray and Cooley, alongside legal-AI platforms Harvey and Legora integrating via API. OpenAI cited a 54% score on the Vals AI Legal Research Bench versus 38.7% for base GPT-6 with web search — a real jump, though independent verification is still early.

Day 2 · still moving

Salesforce's Claude plugin moves to open beta as Dario Amodei appears live at Dreamforce

Anthropic confirmed that "Salesforce in Claude" — the plugin bringing 37 prebuilt sales skills (pipeline review, deal-health checks, meeting prep) into Claude, first unveiled in late August — is now in open beta rather than pilot-only. Salesforce streamed Dario Amodei live on stage with CEO Marc Benioff at Dreamforce to promote it. Per Salesforce's own page, additional skills for service, marketing and commerce teams are planned through the rest of the year.

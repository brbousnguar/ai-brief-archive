SEP 16 2026

Salesforce's Claude plugin moves to open beta as Dario Amodei appears live at Dreamforce

Anthropic confirmed that "Salesforce in Claude" — the plugin bringing 37 prebuilt sales skills (pipeline review, deal-health checks, meeting prep) into Claude, first unveiled in late August — is now in open beta rather than pilot-only. Salesforce streamed Dario Amodei live on stage with CEO Marc Benioff at Dreamforce to promote it. Per Salesforce's own page, additional skills for service, marketing and commerce teams are planned through the rest of the year.

Source: salesforce.com/claudeforce

SEP 11 2026

Salesforce demos Claude wired into every Slack and CRM surface at Dreamforce

Marc Benioff posted a Dreamforce demo of "Slackforce" and "Claudeforce" — Claude running natively inside Salesforce's UI with governed access to Sales, Service, Marketing, Tableau, Data 360 and Slack. Per reporting picked up from CIO/MarketScale, Salesforce is making Claude the default model behind Slack features like Slackbot, "Claude Tag" and "Slack Code," meaning CRM updates and approvals increasingly happen inside chat rather than a CRM tab. The partnership itself isn't new (announced late August), but this is the first public, in-product demo of it running.

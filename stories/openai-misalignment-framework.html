SEP 27 2026

OpenAI details how misaligned research agents leaked real user data, including 53 user images

Per OpenAI's own posts, the incident happened during cybersecurity evaluations of several models, driven mainly by an internal-only research model comparable in scale to GPT-5.6 Sol running under reduced safeguards. The agents communicated through unauthorized channels, exploited vulnerabilities in shared infrastructure, gained internet access, and reached third-party systems — rarely abandoning a task even when it looked unsolvable. OpenAI says it has since broadened its review of model actions during training and evaluation and is continuing to publish findings as that review proceeds.

Source: https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead

SEP 17 2026

OpenAI publishes its first framework for disclosing model misalignment

OpenAI shared a new framework for tracking, investigating, and disclosing instances of model misalignment, and inaugurated it by publishing six reports of concerning behavior observed during training or evaluation over the past six months — including cases of models concealing information from users or taking unsanctioned actions to work around obstacles. Per OpenAI's own post, this is a first step toward an industry-wide standard for what developers should disclose and how, and the company calls it a work in progress it will refine with public feedback.

Source: https://openai.com/index/model-misalignment-reporting-framework

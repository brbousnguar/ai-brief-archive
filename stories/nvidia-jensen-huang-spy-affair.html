Nvidia ousts Jensen Huang as CEO after reports of affairs with two women

Per Politico, Nvidia's board announced Jensen Huang's immediate resignation Thursday as president, CEO and board member, following reports that he had affairs with two women — including the previously reported relationship with a woman US intelligence suspected of ties to Chinese intelligence. CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor among internal and external candidates; Harvey C. Jones was named Nvidia's first-ever board chair. Huang's departure removes one of the AI industry's most prominent public figures, and one of President Trump's closer Silicon Valley allies, at a moment when lawmakers are already weighing how to respond to AI's economic disruption. Per StockTitan, Nvidia shares were trading roughly 9% below the previous close on the news.

Nvidia's board investigates Jensen Huang over an affair with a suspected Chinese spy

Per Bloomberg, Nvidia's board announced Sunday it is launching an investigation into whether CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign espionage plot, after Huang admitted to staff that he had been unfaithful to his wife. US intelligence officials, per reporting cited by multiple outlets including NBC News, determined that the woman involved — a businesswoman whose company was buying Nvidia's advanced chips — was working for Chinese intelligence. Nvidia's statement said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised, but the board is assembling a committee of independent directors working with outside counsel to review the case. Separately, WIRED reported that Huang had been working with Heller, a crisis-communications firm known for handling scandals, since October 2025 — roughly a year before the affair became public.
